### Impact ledger

| Signal | What I delivered | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **€1bn+ in reserves** | Calculated, reconciled, and independently validated more than €1 billion in mathematical reserves across recurring savings and retirement assignments. | Supported regulatory submissions, audit conclusions, and management decisions. |
| **CFO and CRO alignment** | Aligned finance and risk stakeholders to complete Solvency II regulatory submissions. | Connected technical actuarial conclusions with the people accountable for financial and risk decisions. |
| **50+ practising actuaries** | Built [SolvaIIRAG](https://github.com/ISHUKLA/SolvaIIRAG), used by more than 50 practising actuaries to locate articles, verify definitions, and prepare regulatory responses. Its retrieval evaluation achieved **89% Hit@4 across 127 Solvency II questions**. | Reduced the effort required to navigate fragmented regulatory material while keeping answers connected to their sources. |
| **Human-governed AI** | Created the [Excel Audit Agent](https://github.com/ISHUKLA/Excel-Audit-agent), a 494-test workflow that surfaces hard-coded values, formula errors, control breaks, and reconciliation failures. Every conclusion passes through four mandatory human-review gates. [Watch the demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bvdnjimTNCQ). | Gives actuaries a faster first line of review and gives CROs clearer visibility over spreadsheet-related operational risk without delegating professional judgement to AI. |
| **Climate-risk research** | Developed the [Belgian Actuarial Climate Index](https://github.com/ISHUKLA/BACI-climate-index), a reproducible prototype combining five Belgian climate indicators over 1961–2024. | Explores how transparent climate signals could inform SCR analysis and actuarial risk assessment. It remains a research methodology, not a production SCR model. |
